AgentOS

Building a trusted AI companion that works across devices, remembers users over time, and helps get real work done.

What is AgentOS?

AgentOS is an early-stage AI infrastructure project focused on making artificial intelligence safer, more personal, and more useful in everyday life.

We are not building another foundation AI model.

We are building a system that connects users, AI models, applications, and devices — while keeping users in control.

Our Vision

AgentOS focuses on four core capabilities:

1. Security & Privacy

AI should help people without having unrestricted access to their personal information, files, or devices.

2. Cross-Device Intelligence

Phones, computers, smartwatches, cars, and future devices should work together as one intelligent ecosystem.

3. Personal Identity & Long-Term Memory

Users should be able to change devices or AI models without losing their personal context, preferences, and history.

4. Proactive Personal Intelligence

AI should gradually understand users, anticipate their needs, and help prepare tasks — with appropriate permissions and user control.

Current Development

Our first prototype is called JARVIS.

We have successfully tested several foundational capabilities using a real iPhone and Mac:

* Cryptographically signed cross-device tasks.
* Device identity and trust verification.
* Replay protection.
* Data access boundary checks.
* Signed authorization challenges and local signature verification.

The project is still under active development.

These security tests do not mean the complete product is ready.

Our Next Milestone

Our immediate goal is to demonstrate a real workflow:

Phone request → Trusted computer → Authorized data processing → AI-generated document → Phone review

This end-to-end workflow has not yet been completed.

Long-Term Goal

We envision a future where every person can have a trusted AI companion that grows more useful over time.

One identity. Continuous memory. Multiple devices. User control.

The future of AI is not just smarter models. It’s AI that can safely work for people.
